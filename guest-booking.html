Cancellation, rescheduling and refund
  • Cancel: you can cancel online up to the day before your reservation. On the day itself, please contact the management.
  • Reschedule: an approved reservation can be moved once, to another open schedule, before its date. The management must approve the new schedule.
  • Refund: if your reservation is rejected, or your cancellation is accepted by the management, your payment is refunded in full to the account you paid from. You will receive an email when the refund is done.
  • No-shows and cancellations made after the reservation has started are not refunded unless the management decides otherwise.
